ABOUT STUDYFLOW

A Simple Planner for Students

StudyFlow helps students organize their academic tasks, notes, timetable and GPA in one place.

Project Purpose

Students often manage their assignments, notes and class schedules in different places. StudyFlow provides a simple digital workspace where important academic information can be organized and accessed easily.

Main Features

  • Task planner with saved tasks
  • Weekly academic timetable
  • Digital notebook with word counter
  • Notes download feature
  • GPA calculator
  • Browser LocalStorage

Technologies Used
